About Gram Studio

Gram Studio is a digital art studio creating downloadable and printable designs — including printables, notebook covers, bookmarks, stickers, and more. We offer both digital files and select physical products, distributed under clear licensing options for personal and commercial use.

Our Mission

We aim to make thoughtful, well-made digital goods that bring warmth and character to everyday life. Every product is produced with attention to composition, finish, and usability so it prints beautifully and integrates easily into your projects.

What We Make

Our collections focus on small-format, high-quality designs: printable art, notebook and planner covers, bookmarks, tags, sticker sheets, and related assets. Files are delivered print-ready (high-res PDF/PNG) and are available under licensing terms that suit hobbyists, creators, and businesses.

Design & AI

We use AI tools as creative assistants to explore ideas and speed workflows, but our designs are original and reviewed, refined, and finalized by human designers. Products are not fully AI-generated — human authorship and curation are central to our process.

Studio & Process

Work typically starts with concept sketches and style exploration, moves through digital composition, and finishes with color-proofing and export for print. We prioritize responsible sourcing for any physical production and deliver files optimized for common print sizes and formats.

Team

We are a compact team of designers and makers who handle concept, production, licensing, and customer support directly. Staying small helps us keep standards high and provide personal service.

Collaborations, Licensing & Custom Work

We welcome collaborations and offer licensing for use in products, packaging, and campaigns. Custom commissions and bespoke runs are available by request — contact us with project details and intended usage so we can recommend the right license and deliverables.
