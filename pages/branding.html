Typography

  • Headings: "Courier New", Courier, monospace (Bold)
  • Body: ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, "Noto Sans"
  • Use sentence case. Avoid letter-spacing on body copy. Line-height ~1.5.

Usage Guidelines

  • Maintain logo clear space equal to the cap height of “A.”
  • Do not recolor the logo outside the palette. On photos, ensure sufficient contrast.
  • Do not skew, outline, add shadows, or place the logo in a container that reduces legibility.
  • When in doubt, use the primary logo on Paper with Gold accents.
