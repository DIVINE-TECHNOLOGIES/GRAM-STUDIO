License Name: Auric Artisan Personal License

Permitted Use:

  • You may use the artwork for personal, non-commercial projects, such as printing for yourself, home decor, phone wallpapers, or personal gifts.

  • You may showcase it on personal blogs or social media (credit optional, appreciated).

Not Allowed:

  • Reselling, redistributing, or giving away the digital files.

  • Claiming the artwork as your own creation.

Ownership: The copyright remains with Auric Artisan. You are purchasing usage rights, not ownership.

💼 2. Commercial License (Standard License)

License Name: Auric Artisan Commercial License

Permitted Use:

  • Use the artwork in up to 500 end products for sale or promotion (e.g., notebooks, prints, apparel, packaging, or website backgrounds).
  • Use for branding, marketing, or client work, provided the total print run does not exceed 500 copies or downloads.
  • Modify or overlay text and design elements, as long as the result is part of a larger composition.

Not Allowed:

  • Selling, redistributing, or sharing the artwork in its original or modified form as a standalone file.
  • Using the artwork in on-demand products (e.g., Printify, Redbubble) without adding your own design elements.
  • Registering the artwork or its derivatives as trademarks or exclusive rights.

Attribution: Credit is optional but appreciated: Design by Auric Artisan.

🏢 3. Extended Commercial License (Unlimited License)

License Name: Auric Artisan Extended Commercial License

Permitted Use:

  • Use the artwork for unlimited end products, digital or physical.
  • Suitable for large-scale or commercial distribution, such as merchandise, product lines, advertising campaigns, book covers, digital wallpapers, and resale in high quantities.
  • Use in print-on-demand products, provided you significantly modify or integrate it into a unique design.
  • Use across multiple brands, businesses, or clients owned by you or your organization.

Not Allowed:

  • Reselling, distributing, or bundling the artwork as a raw or editable file.
